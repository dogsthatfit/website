How the month runs

  • Before they arrive

    A first call to set up your home, your routine and your shopping list.

  • Week 1: settling in

    Sleep, toilet training and a routine that works for your household.

  • Week 2: building habits

    Handling, bitey moments and short spells on their own.

  • Week 3: the wider world

    Confident socialisation, new noises and early recall games.

  • Week 4: making it stick

    Pulling it all together and planning the months ahead.

Questions

My puppy is older than 16 weeks. Can I still join?

If they've only just come home, get in touch and we'll talk it through. For dogs from about five months, the Adolescent Reset is usually a better fit.

Does remote training really work for puppies?

Yes. Most of what makes a difference happens between sessions, when you're practising at home. Remote support means you get help while it's happening, not just once a week.

When does my 30 days start?

On the day your puppy comes home. If you book before then, we'll use the time to get your home ready.

How much time will it take each day?

A few short sessions of a few minutes each, worked into things you already do, like meals and play.

What happens after day 30?

You keep the full guide library. If you'd like more help as your puppy grows, we can talk about what comes next.

Puppy coming home soon?

Book a free 15-minute call. Tell me about your puppy and your home, and we'll work out the right start together.
