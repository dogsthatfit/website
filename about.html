About me

Hi, I'm Sarah

I'm a force-free dog trainer based in Cheltenham, and I help new puppy owners get through those first weeks without losing their minds (or their shoes).

I live with five dogs, the newest a Chinese Crested puppy, so the 3am toilet trips are very fresh. Raising her reminded me of something I tell every client: knowing what to do is the easy bit. Doing it, a little every day, is what changes things.

So my guides are short and practical, and my programmes are built around practice. You do the work at home, and I'm there to check in, watch your videos and help when it gets wobbly.
