Privacy policy

Dogs That Fit is run by Sarah McConnell as a sole trader in Cheltenham. This page explains what I do with your details.

What I collect

If you send a message or book a call, I keep your name, email, phone number if given, and what you tell me about your dog. Bookings are handled by TidyCal and guide purchases by Payhip, who have their own privacy policies.

Why

To reply to you, run the training you've booked, and send programme emails. I don't sell or share your details for marketing.

How long

Client records are kept for as long as I need them for training and my business records, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing dogsthatfit@gmail.com.

Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
